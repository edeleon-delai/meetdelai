4. How Antes are settled

Whether an Ante is won is decided by our server against its own clock, and where relevant against the health readings you have authorised. The device does not get to declare a win. This is deliberate: a number that can be edited by the person it describes is not worth having.

We may refuse to settle an Ante as won where we reasonably believe the result was fabricated or the app was manipulated.
