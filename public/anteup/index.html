Today is decision 1,001.

The apps you lose time to stay shut by default. Not blocked on a timer, not guilt-tripped — shut, until you earn them back.

Put up an Ante against one of them. Go for a walk, read, train, focus. The minutes you earn are the minutes they open for. Nothing resets, nothing is taken away, and there is no streak to break.

How it works

  1. Pick the app you want back. iOS holds the list — we never learn which apps you chose, only how many.
  2. Put up an Ante. Choose what you'll do instead, and for how long.
  3. Do the thing. Apple Health settles a walk or a workout. The camera can count reps if you'd rather not wear anything.
  4. Collect. Your apps open for what you earned. Or save it and spend it later.

What counts

Your thingYou decide

Activity counts whether or not you declared it. A walk you took this morning is still a walk.
