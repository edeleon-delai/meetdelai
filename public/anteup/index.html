How it works
01

Pick the prize

Choose the apps you actually want to earn — the feed, the streaming, the game. AnteUp reads your installed apps and locks only what you pick.

02

Name your price

What would you rather be doing? Read, walk, focus, exercise, sleep — or write your own. You set the exchange rate, not an algorithm.

03

Accept the ante

One clean trade, stated plainly before you start: what you put in, what you collect. Accept it and the clock runs.

04

Prove it, then collect

Prop up the phone and move — the camera counts the work. When the ante is paid, the app unlocks for exactly as long as you bought.
